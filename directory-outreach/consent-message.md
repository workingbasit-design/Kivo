# EveryJob Directory — Consent Outreach Messages

Send one business at a time via WhatsApp/SMS. Replace the bracketed bits. Only list a business after it replies YES.

## English (Canada)

> Hi [Name]! I'm building EveryJob Directory — a free listing of trusted local pros in Canada. Customers search for services like [plumbing / electrical / cleaning / appliance repair] and send quote requests straight to you. Completely free, no commissions, no catch. Want in? Just reply YES and I'll add [Business Name].

## After a YES

Once they agree, collect: business name, service category, service area/city, and the phone number they want shown publicly — then add them to EveryJob Directory yourself (register the business and opt it into the directory). Never publish details they didn't confirm.
